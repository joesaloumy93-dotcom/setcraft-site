Setcraft

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Setcraft collects nothing. Everything you do stays on your iPhone.

Setcraft is a workout timer. It is built to keep everything on your phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any information about you.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app is saved only on your own iPhone, in the app's private storage:

  • the workouts and exercises you create
  • your workout history: dates, times, sets done and reps counted
  • your favourite quotes and the quotes you have already seen
  • the awards you create yourself
  • your settings, such as voice, vibration and which quotes you see

None of it is sent to me or to anyone else. I cannot see it.

No accounts, no tracking, no advertising

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that tracks you.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or notifications. It reads Apple Health only if you turn on "Count steps" (below).

Voice and Lock Screen

The spoken cues use the voices built into your iPhone and work offline. The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island timer is updated by the app on your phone; nothing is sent over the internet.

Steps from Apple Health

If you turn on "Count steps" in Settings, the app asks to read three things from Apple Health: steps, walking + running distance, and flights climbed. It reads them for the time of your own workouts (kept in your workout history on this iPhone) and for today's total, shown at the top of History. It never writes to Health. Health data is never sent anywhere, used for advertising, or shared with anyone. It is off by default; you can turn it off in the app's Settings, and remove access in the Health app (your picture → Apps → Setcraft).

Rep counting with the phone underneath you

If you turn it on, the app uses the iPhone's front proximity sensor (the one that darkens the screen during calls) to count push-ups. The sensor only reports "something is near" or "nothing is near". No camera, no microphone, no images and no recordings are used or kept. It can be turned off in the app's Settings.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can start or pause a workout by asking Siri. Siri is run by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; the app never hears your voice. It only receives the request (for example "start Full Body"), and Siri only receives your workout names from the app, never your history. It can be turned off in the app's Settings, or in iPhone Settings → Apps → Setcraft → Siri.

You decide what is on

Features that need your permission from iOS start switched off: steps from Apple Health and the Lock Screen timer. Everything else (voice, Siri, the push-up sensor, quotes, custom awards) starts on, and every one of them can be turned off in Settings.

Privacy on screen

When you leave the app, it covers its own screen, so the app switcher never shows your workouts or history. If you use the Lock Screen timer, you can hide workout and exercise names there with "Show names on Lock Screen".

Backup files you export

In Settings you can export a backup: one file with your workouts, history, custom awards and favourite quotes. You choose where it is saved (for example On My iPhone, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). The app never uploads it anywhere itself. If you save it to a cloud service, that service's privacy policy applies to the file. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Backups

Your phone may include the app's data in its own backups, such as an iCloud or computer backup, as it does for most apps. That is handled by Apple under your own settings. The app itself does not create or send backups.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your phone deletes everything it saved. Inside the app, Settings → Your data has "Delete everything" (workouts, history, awards, favourites and settings, as if the app were just installed). Turning off "Count steps" offers to remove steps already saved. Health and Siri access are controlled by iOS: change them in the Health app and iPhone Settings.

Children

The app does not collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new policy will be published here with a new date, and the app's App Store page will say what changed.
